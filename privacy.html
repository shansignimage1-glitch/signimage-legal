PRIVACY POLICY FOR HERMES SIGN IMAGE CC QUICKBOOKS INTEGRATION APPLICATION Effective Date: 29/08/2026 Last Updated: 29/08/2026 1. INTRODUCTION Sign Image CC (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal and business data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard information when you use the Hermes Sign Image CC QuickBooks Integration Application (the “Application” or “Software”), which facilitates data exchange between your internal business systems (e.g., Hermes, Sign Image CC operational tools) and your QuickBooks Online account. By using the Application, you consent to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Application. 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT We may collect the following types of information: Category Examples Purpose Account & Authentication Data Intuit OAuth tokens (access token, refresh token), associated realm ID (Company ID), Intuit user ID or email used to authorize the connection. To establish and maintain a secure connection between the Application and your QuickBooks Online account. Business & Financial Data Customer names, contact info, vendor details, item/service lists, invoices, estimates, bills, purchase orders, payments, journal entries, chart of accounts, reports, time‑tracking data, job‑cost data, inventory quantities, etc. To synchronize data between your internal systems and QuickBooks Online (e.g., pulling invoices for reporting, pushing job‑costs as expenses, updating customer/vendor records). Usage & Technical Data IP address, browser/user agent, timestamps, API request/response logs, error messages, performance metrics. To monitor service health, troubleshoot issues, improve performance, and ensure security. Communications Messages you send to our support team (e‑mail, chat, ticketing system). To provide customer support and respond to inquiries. We do not collect sensitive personal data such as racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, health data, biometric data, or sexual orientation unless you voluntarily provide it in the context of business data (e.g., a customer’s name that happens to include such information). 3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION We use the collected information to: Enable the core functionality of the Application (authenticating with QuickBooks, reading/writing data). Provide and improve the Service – including troubleshooting, bug fixes, performance enhancements, and feature development. Send service‑related communications (e.g., security notices, maintenance alerts, updates to this Policy). Respond to your support requests and improve customer service. Comply with legal obligations (e.g., responding to lawful requests, preventing fraud). Analyze usage trends in an aggregated, anonymized form to guide product decisions. We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal or business data to third parties for marketing purposes. 4. SHARING AND DISCLOSURE We may share your information only in the following circumstances: With Intuit (QuickBooks) – solely as necessary to facilitate the OAuth connection and API calls you authorize (e.g., sending invoices, retrieving customer lists). With Service Providers – trusted third parties that help us operate the Application (e.g., cloud hosting, monitoring, error‑tracking, email delivery). These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only to provide services to us. For Legal Reasons – if we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law, regulation, litigation, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Sign Image CC, our users, or others. With Your Consent – any other sharing will be done only after obtaining your explicit consent. We implement appropriate contractual and technical safeguards to ensure that any third party handling your data provides at least the same level of protection as this Policy requires. 5. DATA RETENTION We retain your data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (e.g., tax or accounting regulations). OAuth tokens are stored encrypted and refreshed as needed; revoked tokens are deleted promptly. Logs and usage data are kept for a maximum of 12 months, after which they are deleted or aggregated/anonymized. Upon your request to disconnect the Application from your QuickBooks account, we will delete all stored tokens and cease further data synchronization. Residual logs may be retained for a short period for audit/security reasons, then deleted. 6. DATA SECURITY We employ a variety of industry‑standard security measures to protect your data, including but not limited to: Transport Encryption: All API calls to Intuit and our servers use TLS 1.2 or higher. Data at Rest: Sensitive data (tokens, credentials) are stored encrypted using strong algorithms (AES‑256). Access Controls: Access to production systems is limited to authorized personnel using role‑based permissions and multi‑factor authentication. Regular Audits: We conduct periodic security reviews, vulnerability scans, and penetration testing. Incident Response: We maintain an incident‑response plan to promptly detect, contain, and remediate any security events. While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. You acknowledge that you use the Application at your own risk regarding data security. 7.
